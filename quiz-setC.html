Set C – Sequencing & CRISPR

Learning Mode Exam Mode

1. What major defect is visible in ms8 anther histology (Fig. 4)?

Tapetum defects directly cause pollen abortion.

2. Why does the sterile sector lack viable pollen?

MS8 function is essential for anther development.

3. What type of mutation is Q111*?

Stop codons truncate essential protein domains.

4. What type of mutation is P98S?

Missense changes can impair TF activity.

5. What does it mean that P98S and Q111* are “in cis”?

Cis linkage is key to Model C’s prediction.

6. Why were sterile sectors sequenced rather than whole plants?

Sector-only mutations reveal new alleles.

7. What does the IGV visualization in Fig. 5 show?

Reads confirm allele specificity to sterile sectors.

8. Why is sequencing depth important in mutation validation?

Low coverage = unreliable variant calling.

9. What phenotype did CRISPR alleles (ms8-3, ms8-4) produce?

Phenocopy = direct causal proof.

10. Why is CRISPR considered the strongest evidence for MS8 being causal?

Novel alleles reproduce the sterile phenotype.

11. Why were sterile and fertile sectors from the same panicle compared?

Same plant = perfect control.

12. Why was the ms8-5 allele useful?

Independent allele = strong validation.

13. What is the functional consequence of losing the bHLH domain?

bHLH domains bind E-box sequences.

14. Why do sterile sectors contain mutations not found in fertile sectors?

Somatic mutation → mosaic sector phenotype.

15. Why does sector sequencing reduce the search space for causal mutations?

Only sterile tissues hold causative alleles.

16. What sequencing evidence rules out structural variants?

No SV signatures in mapped region.

17. What conclusion is supported by the presence of two mutations in cis?

Cis = same chromosome copy mutated.

18. Why is CRISPR validation particularly convincing in this paper?

Independent evidence for causality.

19. What part of sequencing evidence shows ms8 mutation was not inherited from the parent?

New allele = EMS-induced.

20. Why does the paper compare multiple allele types (EMS, CRISPR, natural)?

Allelic series is gold-standard functional validation.

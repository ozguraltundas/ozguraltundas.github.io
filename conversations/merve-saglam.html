Interview with Merve Sağlam

Merve Sağlam is a PhD candidate at Texas A&M University, working on cowpea breeding and genetics. She completed her B.S. in Molecular Biology and Genetics at Bilecik Şeyh Edebali University in 2016 and earned her M.S. in Biotechnology from Eskişehir Technical University in 2020. After two years in the private sector focusing on cell culture and real-time PCR applications, she was awarded the YLSY Scholarship in 2019 and began her PhD in 2022. Within the scope of the program, she is affiliated with Selçuk University.

On Motivation and Academic Journey

Özgür: What motivated you to pursue a PhD in the United States?

Merve: When I was five or six years old, I watched a news report about a PhD student conducting research in the United States. Since then, I have consistently worked toward that goal.

On Current Research

Özgür: What project are you currently working on?

Merve: I am working on cowpea breeding and genetics, focusing on improving traits related to crop performance and resilience.

On Mentorship

Özgür: What kind of feedback does your supervisor give you?

Merve: My supervisor, Dr. Endang Septiningsih, is very supportive and encourages academic and personal growth.

On Success and Professional Philosophy

Özgür: What does success mean to you?

Merve: Success means feeling fulfilled and contributing meaningfully rather than focusing solely on numerical metrics.

On Future Plans

Özgür: Academia or industry?

Merve: I plan to return to Türkiye and work as an assistant professor, possibly after a postdoctoral position.

Özgür: Thank you for your time.

Merve: You are very welcome.

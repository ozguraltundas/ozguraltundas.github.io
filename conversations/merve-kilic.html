Academic Talks · Episode 02

Merve Kılıç

Crop Production & Seed Growing

Crop Production Manager · Prof Farm

LinkedIn

Career Pathways & Industry Insights

Merve Kılıç’s contribution to Episode 02 focuses on crop production management and seed growing, with particular attention to seed vigour, genetic purity, and germination rate.

These illustrative answers were written for this page and are not guest quotations.

1. On Career Pathways

Özgür: What motivated you to pursue a career in crop production management, and how has your academic training influenced the way you approach practical production challenges?

Example response: Crop production connects scientific knowledge with visible results in the field. Academic training supports careful observation and evidence-based decisions, while practical experience builds judgement. Understanding both the crop and the team is essential for effective management.

2. On Seed Performance

Özgür: How do you evaluate seed vigour, genetic purity, and germination rate together when making decisions about seed growing and production quality?

Example response: Germination measures the ability to produce normal seedlings under favourable test conditions. Vigour helps explain performance across a wider range of conditions, while genetic purity concerns varietal identity. These measures should be considered together with production history and the intended sowing environment.

3. On Field Decisions

Özgür: How do you adjust production plans when weather, crop performance, or field conditions change, while keeping your team aligned with quality goals?

Example response: Use field observations and weather information to identify the most urgent tasks. Update the schedule, clarify responsibilities and explain changes through short team briefings. Check the crop’s response afterwards so that further adjustments are based on evidence.

4. On Professional Development

Özgür: For a young graduate entering crop or seed production, which practical experiences and skills would you prioritise during the first few years of their career?

Example response: Gain experience throughout the production cycle and spend time with seed-testing teams. Prioritise crop observation, sampling, field records and basic spreadsheet skills. Clear communication helps ensure that useful observations lead to timely decisions.

Further reading: Niab seed quality tests and Maize Research Institute seed quality analyses.
